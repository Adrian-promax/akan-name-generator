The Akan are a major West African ethnolinguistic group, primarily residing in Ghana and parts of Côte d'Ivoire, comprising nearly half of Ghana's population. They are renowned for their matrilineal society, rich gold-trading history, and vibrant art, including Akan gold weights, spanning subgroups such as the Asante, Fante, Akuapem, and Baule
